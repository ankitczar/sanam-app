Sanam

Your study. Your syllabus. Your revisions. Your progress.

Sanam is a student-focused study and productivity app built to help you plan, study, track, revise, and understand your progress — all in one place.

It was designed with competitive-exam students in mind, with NEET as the primary focus.

«Plan it. Study it. Track it. Revise it.»

---

✨ What is Sanam?

Studying for a competitive exam isn't just about studying more.

You also need to know:

- What should I study today?
- What have I already completed?
- What do I need to revise?
- Which chapters are weak?
- How consistent have I been?
- How much of my syllabus is actually complete?
- How many lectures and DPPs have I finished?
- Am I improving month by month?

Sanam brings these pieces together into one student-focused system.

---

📚 Core Features

🗓️ Planner

Plan your study days, tasks, revisions, and goals.

Sanam's planner is designed to help turn a large syllabus into manageable daily work.

🔄 Custom Revision Cycles

Create revision schedules that match the way you study.

Use predefined cycles or create your own custom intervals — whether you want to revise after 1 day, 5 days, 15 days, 30 days, or any schedule that works for you.

⏳ Countdown

Keep important dates visible with countdowns for exams and other goals.

Know exactly how much time you have left.

📖 Syllabus Tracker

Track your syllabus subject-by-subject and chapter-by-chapter.

See what is:

- Not started
- In progress
- Completed
- Due for revision

🎥 Lecture & DPP Tracker

For students studying through online platforms, track:

- Lectures
- DPPs
- Classes
- Completion status

Keep your digital coursework connected to your overall study progress.

📝 Practice Tracker

Track your practice and question-solving progress alongside your syllabus and revision.

📊 Progress Tracker

See your overall preparation at a glance.

Track completion and progress across subjects and areas of study.

📈 Study Analytics

Understand how you actually study.

Sanam can provide insights into your study activity over time, including monthly study analysis and progress trends.

The goal isn't to give you meaningless numbers.

It's to help answer:

«“Am I actually moving forward?”»

---

🎯 Focus Mode

When it's time to study, Sanam can switch from planning to execution.

Start a focused study session and concentrate on the task you're working on.

App-blocking and additional distraction-control features are being developed to make Focus Mode more powerful.

---

📱 Widgets

Keep your preparation visible without opening the app.

Sanam includes customizable widgets designed around useful study information such as:

- Exam countdowns
- Progress
- Study information
- Revision information
- Goals

More widget customization is planned.

---

🎨 Themes & Customization

Make Sanam feel like your study space.

Customize the experience with themes and other visual options.

Sanam is designed to stay personal without becoming unnecessarily complicated.

---

🔔 Notifications

Get reminders for the things that actually matter:

- Upcoming revisions
- Planned study tasks
- Important dates
- Other customizable reminders

Notification timing and behavior can be customized.

---

💾 Your Data

Sanam supports data import and export so that you can keep control of your study data.

The goal is to make your progress portable rather than locking it away.

---

🎓 Built for Students

Sanam was originally created around the experience of preparing for competitive examinations.

The first major focus is NEET, but the underlying system is designed to support other students and examinations as the project grows.
